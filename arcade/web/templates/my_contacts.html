What a tag does and does not promise
A tag points where its holder points it, and nowhere else. Nobody can move your tag to their own address while you hold it. What you can do is let go of it: claiming a different name frees the one you have now the moment the change is mined, and the next person to claim it gets it — along with anything anyone sends to that name from then on. That is worth knowing before you change it, because the people who have your tag written down will not hear that you did. Somebody who saved you here is safe: this book stores the address a tag named, not the name.

First claim wins. A tag belongs to whoever claimed it first and nobody else can take it. What a claim proves is control of an address at that moment — it says nothing about who they are, so confirm a name with the person before you trust it with anything that matters.
