What one account may do here

Counted per account, over a rolling hour, and shown to them on their own page before they reach it. Zero closes an action completely and says so — nothing of anybody’s is lost by that, they can do it on another node or their own.

A post, a reaction, a message and a send are each one transaction. A block comes about a minute after the last one, so thirty an hour is one every two minutes, held all day — more than anybody writes prose at, less than a bot needs. An inscription is counted smaller because it is the one that stays: a post is answered by the next post, and an inscription is a piece of the chain this node carries forever. A token issuance is counted apart from inscriptions rather than with them, because a name on the chain cannot be taken back either, and a run of pictures should not cost somebody their go at one. The bytes are what one person may make this node store and carry forever. A trade — an offer on somebody’s piece, or an order on the exchange — spends a fee and reserves nothing, so it is counted with the other things that are answered rather than with the ones that stay.

What this node takes of a trade it made the offer for — a sale from your shop, a fill of your order, an offer you accepted. The buyer pays it on top of the price, so an ask of 100 still means the seller receives 100. It travels in the offer, so it is on the page before anybody signs, and a percentage too small to be an output is not charged at all. Zero, which is where this starts, asks nothing of anybody.
