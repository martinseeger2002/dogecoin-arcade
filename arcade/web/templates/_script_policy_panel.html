Script policy

An unlocked wallet’s words are kept in the browser while you use it, so a script slipped into one of this arcade’s pages could read them. With this on, a browser runs only this arcade’s own scripts on its pages and refuses any other: an injected <script>, an on…= attribute or a javascript: link. What it refuses is written to this node’s log. Inscriptions and games keep their own sandbox and are not changed by it.

{% if script_policy.forced %}On for every page: the node’s service turns it on (--enforce-script-policy or ARCADE_ENFORCE_SCRIPT_POLICY), so this switch cannot turn it off.{% elif script_policy.enforce %}On: pages are sent the enforced policy.{% else %}Off: pages are only measured against it, and the browser reports what it would refuse.{% endif %}
