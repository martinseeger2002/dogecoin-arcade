Edition

Full runs everything. ARCADE Lite runs the crypto cores only (the chains and their wallets, sync and borrowing, send and receive, the address book, accounts, settings, help and the mesh link) and opens on the Lite window. Changing the edition sets every module to that edition's default. Nothing is deleted: a module switched off keeps its data for when it is on again.
