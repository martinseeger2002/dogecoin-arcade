Full arcade or ARCADE Lite?

Full is everything this arcade does: the feed, messages, the exchange, ords and Explore, apps and games. ARCADE Lite starts with the crypto cores only: the chains and their wallets, send and receive, the address book, accounts, settings, help and the mesh link. Anything else can be switched on later, and nothing is deleted when something is switched off.
