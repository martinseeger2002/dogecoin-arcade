Inscribe a collection

Choose a HashLips Art Engine build folder (its images and json, or json/_metadata.json), or any folder of pictures. You approve once; every picture then goes on as its own ord, one after another, signed in this tab. When the last one is in, the collection index — each piece's name, number and traits from its JSON — is inscribed on Pepecoin test, where the arcades index it, and the collection appears on the Exchange. If the tab closes, choose the folder again: pieces already inscribed are skipped and a half-done one carries on.

Make a collection index

Group your ords into a collection the way the Doginal Dogs, the Doginal Frogs and the Anti Doginal Dogs are: an index inscribed by your account names each ord, its number and its traits, and every arcade shows the collection on the Exchange. The first index to name an ord owns it; only you can replace yours. Step by step.
