Make a new wallet

Write the words down before you go on. There is no reset, no recovery and nobody to ask: the words are the wallet.

    Afterwards this site unlocks with your password. The one page that asks for these words is Restore, reached from Sign in when the password is gone, and it never sends them; anything else that asks — to verify, to migrate, for any reason — is stealing them.

    Now type words back in, in that order, separated by spaces — so that what you wrote down is what is on the screen.

    The password encrypts the words in this browser's own storage. It is never sent anywhere, so it cannot be reset — and it protects the copy on this machine, not the wallet itself. A weak one matters less here than on the sign-up page: this copy never leaves the machine, so breaking it means having the machine already.
