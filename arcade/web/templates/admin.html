Switches

{# Off by default (2026-10-13): trades only while both people's tabs are open, signed in each browser; on, this node also finishes trades for people who left. #}

Off (the default): every trade happens while both people have their tab open, built and signed in their own browsers, so this node is trusted with nothing. On: this node also holds pre-signed listings and bids to finish later, fills “while I'm away” buy orders, and may run the custodial mainnet PEP swap below — convenient, but people then trust this node to finish their trades. Lock-script swaps between chains are trustless either way (see Lock-script swaps).

Content screening

A model (vLLM, or any OpenAI-compatible server that reads images) looks at pictures and posts before this arcade shows them. Leave the address empty and nothing is screened: everything shows.

{# 2026-10-17: what this arcade's model flags, told to every arcade (arcade/flags.py). #}

Each picture or post your model flags goes out as a public message every arcade reads: only its fingerprint and the verdict, never the content. Flags are gathered and sent together, a testnet transaction at a time, from this node’s fee address. Other operators choose whether to use them.

Sensitive-material flags from other arcades

Arcades that broadcast what their own screening flagged. Turn one on and what it flags is covered here too (sensitive) or hidden (illegal), even with your own screening off. Their flags can only make this arcade stricter, and anything you clear yourself stays cleared.

No arcade has broadcast flags yet.

Mainnet PEP swap

Trade testnet assets (tokens, testnet coins, NFTs) for Pepecoin on mainnet. This node holds both sides while an order stands and pays them out itself, taking 0.5% of the PEP of each trade. Not trustless: every page that offers it tells people they are trusting you, the operator.

Keep 1 to 2 PEP of your own in the mainnet wallet for network fees: a payout waits rather than spend what is held for somebody else. Saved with Save switches.

Lock-script swaps

Swaps between two chains (PEP for DOGE and the like), settled by lock scripts: trustless -- nobody holds anybody's coins. People's tabs hand this node their own pre-signed claims and refunds so a swap finishes with the tab closed; it can only send those, paying the person who signed them.
