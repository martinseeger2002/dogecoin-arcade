{# What this page deliberately does not have, said rather than left as a gap: Overview's auto-sell, auto-fill, update and scan settings all configure the NODE's own automation, which runs on the node's own key. An account has no such automation to configure -- accepting an offer, filling a bid, or anything else that happens without a fresh signature would need a key on this server that could spend on its own, which is the one thing this design refuses to give it. Every action an account takes is signed here, in this browser, at the moment it is taken. #}
Why is there no automatic selling, no update settings, no scan button?

Those are on the operator's Overview because they run against the node's own key, automatically, without anybody pressing anything at the moment they happen. Doing the same for an account would mean this server holding a key that can spend on its own -- which is exactly the thing a non-custodial account refuses to give it. So there is nothing here that spends without a signature you make at the time.
