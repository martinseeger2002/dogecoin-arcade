Your twelve words

The words are the wallet. This arcade keeps only your wallet sealed with your password, which it cannot open; lose the password with the words not written down and nobody can bring them back.

    {#- 2026-11-09 (security review): the words' coin type, as the website's Backup says it (my_backup.html). -#}

A wallet that expects a coin's registered number (DigiByte 20, Litecoin 2) shows an empty wallet until it is pointed at coin type 3. Your coins are not lost: the recovery page lists each chain's path and keys.
